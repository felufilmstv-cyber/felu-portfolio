Project 02 — AI / LLM

The queue stops
being a queue.

LLM classificationEmbeddingsPythonPostgresRole: solo build

200+ tickets/week hit a shared inbox with no routing. I built a confidence-gated classifier: high-confidence tickets auto-route with a drafted reply; uncertain ones queue for humans with a suggested label.

  triage queue — live dashboard
AUTO-ROUTED 62% · DRAFTED · HUMAN REVIEW 38%

PROBLEM

No priority signal — urgent outages waited behind password resets. First response depended on who glanced at the inbox first.

PROCESS

Labelled 1,000+ historical tickets, built embedding retrieval + LLM classifier with a confidence threshold tuned on a holdout set. Human-in-the-loop from day one: every auto-action logged with its reasoning, one-click undo.

RESULT → 60% auto-routed with drafts; first-response down from hours to under 30 minutes. Support staff handle edge cases, not triage.
